Data Anonymizer
Your data never leaves your browser

Anonymize a spreadsheet of real people

Upload a CSV or Excel file, tell it which columns hold personal details, and get back two files: one with realistic fake data and one with the originals. Both share an anon_key so the same person always maps the same way, and the tool proves the pair can be merged back before you download.
